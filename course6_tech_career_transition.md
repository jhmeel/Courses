# Course 6: Tech Career Transition Course (Non-Tech to Tech)

## Course Description

This comprehensive course is designed to guide individuals from non-technical backgrounds through the exciting and often challenging journey of transitioning into the technology industry. We will cover everything from understanding the tech landscape and identifying your transferable skills, to choosing a suitable tech path, acquiring new skills, building a compelling portfolio, networking effectively, and navigating the job search process. This course aims to empower you with the knowledge, strategies, and confidence to successfully launch a fulfilling career in tech.

## Prerequisites

*   A strong desire to transition into the technology industry.
*   Willingness to learn, adapt, and step outside your comfort zone.
*   Basic computer literacy and internet research skills.
*   No prior technical background is required.

    *   **Self-Paced Online Courses:**
        *   Platforms: Coursera, Udemy, edX, freeCodeCamp, Khan Academy, Udacity, Skillshare, LinkedIn Learning.

        *   Intensive, structured programs (full-time or part-time).

        *   University or college programs.
        *   Pros: Deep theoretical understanding, recognized credentials.
        *   Cons: Time-consuming, expensive, may not be necessary for all roles.

        *   Joining study groups, online communities (Discord, Slack).
